What Heartpact is

Heartpact is the couples app where promises ring. The camera counts the reps, and a heart only fills when you both keep your word.

Free for both of you, on Android.

One dare, two phones

On your phone

  1. Pick the dare. 20 squats at 7:00, or a 60-second plank.
  2. Watch it live. The count climbs on your phone as they go.
  3. Let them off, if you want. Only you can say yes when they ask.

On theirs

  1. It rings. A real alarm, over the lock screen.
  2. The camera counts. Only proper reps, with a tip when one doesn't count.
  3. The heart sticks. Their half lands next to yours.

Fair play, built in.

Reps are counted on the phone, never recorded. Half reps don't count. And if anything looks off, the other half of the heart hears about it.

  • Form, not flailing"Go lower." "Keep your body in one straight line." A rep done badly doesn't count, and the tip says why.
  • Surprise check-insMid-set the screen says "Freeze!". A video on another screen can't do that on cue.
  • Nothing leaves the phoneThe camera reads your pose and lets each frame go. No video, no photos, no upload.

Questions

Is the camera recording me?

No. The phone reads your pose frame by frame to count reps, then lets each frame go. No video or photo from a workout leaves your phone.

What if I'm ill or injured?

Ask your partner to call it off from the alarm, with a note. If they agree, it stops for both of you. You can also silence the alarm on your own phone.

Can my partner see everything I do in the app?

They see what you share as a couple: dares, the album, answers once you've both answered. Food shows as totals, and cycle and location only when you turn sharing on.

Do we both need Android?

Yes, for now: Heartpact is on Google Play.

Who pays for Plus?

Either of you. One subscription covers the couple, so the other phone gets Plus straight away.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Us, or on our account deletion page. It's immediate and permanent.
