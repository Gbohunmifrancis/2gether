Heartpact helps the two of you keep your promises to each other: workouts done, meals logged, questions answered, days spent together.

DARE EACH OTHER TO MOVE
• Set a dare for your partner: the exercise, the reps and the time it rings.
• Squats, push-ups, sit-ups, jumping jacks, plank, arm raises and high knees.
• When it's time, their phone rings. It keeps ringing until the reps are done, for up to 30 minutes.
• Ending a dare early takes both of you to agree. Silencing the alarm on the phone is always one tap away.

THE CAMERA COUNTS THE REPS
• Do the reps in front of the camera and Heartpact counts them on screen.
• Your partner sees when the alarm is ringing and gets your count when you finish.
• The counting happens on your phone.

EVERY PROMISE YOU KEEP BECOMES A HEART
• You stick your half on when you set the dare. Your partner's half sticks when the camera counts the last rep.
• A full heart for every dare done together, on a sticker page you both share.

ONE QUESTION A DAY
• A new question every day, from small talk to big plans.
• Your partner's answer shows once you've answered too.

PLAY TOGETHER, EVEN APART
• Same answer, Guess my answer, Would you rather, Trivia, Truth or dare, Draw and guess, I call on, Word dash, Memory match, Tic-tac-toe and Connect four.
• Invite your partner and play on your own phones, wherever you are.

THE REST OF YOUR DAYS, TOGETHER
• Food: snap a meal and get a calorie estimate shown as a range, because a photo can't be exact. Choose what your partner sees.
• Cycle and pregnancy: track your cycle, appointments and kicks, and decide what your partner sees. Predictions are estimates, not medical advice.
• Map: see each other only while you both share. Either of you can pause in one tap.
• Album: photos stay on your phone until you tap Sync, then they're in your shared album. On this day brings back the ones from past years.

NOTHING IS SHARED UNTIL YOU CHOOSE
Everything starts private. You can always see what your partner can see, and change it in one tap. Your location is never shared one way.

HEARTPACT PLUS
One partner's Plus covers you both: 20 food scans a day instead of 3, 2 GB of shared album instead of 500 MB, and 90 days of question history instead of 7.

Heartpact speaks English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Arabic and Hindi.
